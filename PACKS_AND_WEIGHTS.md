# FIFA 14 local pack catalogue — v2.41.1 BETA 2.25.0

Pack sizes, player-slot counts and advertised rare counts remain the existing FIFA 14 local catalogue. BETA 2.25.0 changes the **draw probabilities**, not the Store pack definitions.

## Gold base-rating tuning

- 75–79: 72.0
- 80–83: 20.0
- 84–86: 6.0
- 87–89: 1.7
- 90+: 0.3

Rare player slots and promo-style packs apply additional multipliers to the upper bands. A regular pack is capped at two 90+ players. Every supported base player remains eligible inside the correct Bronze/Silver/Gold quality pool.

## Special jackpot probabilities

The special roll happens once per pack. If the first special hits, a separate much-smaller conditional roll can place a second special. `maxSpecialsPerPack` is hard-capped at 2.

- Gold Pack: 8%
- Premium Gold Pack: 16%
- Jumbo Premium Gold Pack: 25%
- Premium Gold Players Pack: 35%
- Mega Pack: 45%
- Rare Players Pack: 60%
- Jumbo Rare Players / 100k Pack: 70%

Conditional second-special chances range from 1% in ordinary Gold packs up to 5% in the 100k pack. Therefore a double-special 100k pack remains far less common than a single-special pack.

Within the special-card pool, rating receives a second scarcity taper. Low/mid IF cards are deliberately obtainable; elite 90+, TOTY and equivalent cards remain substantially rarer so the local Transfer Market retains meaningful scarcity.

World Cup-only cards are excluded from normal FUT packs. Legend pack odds remain 0% until the PC client-side Legend identity/art path is proven.

## Consumables

The 151-definition consumable union catalogue remains enabled, with 128 proven definitions eligible for normal pack generation. Bronze/Silver utility slots focus on contracts, fitness, healing and training; Gold also includes mapped position, chemistry-style and manager-league consumables.

The 99-match player contract is applyable but not a normal pack pull. The unresolved asset-35/asset-43 definitions remain held out rather than guessed.

## Balance intent

BETA 2.25.0 is intentionally more accessible than original-era FUT 14. The target is for players to build strong teams without needing original-2014 grind levels, while preserving enough rarity at the elite end that high-rated cards and desirable specials still have meaningful Transfer Market prices.
